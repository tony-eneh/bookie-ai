How it works

Less bookkeeping. More knowing.

BookieAI does the boring part and only asks you when it genuinely needs to.

  1. 01

    Log it in seconds

    A couple of taps puts a transaction on the right account, in the right currency and category.

  2. 02

    Bookie asks, you answer

    When something is unclear, you get one quick question instead of a spreadsheet to fix. Answer with a tap.

  3. 03

    Ask anything, anytime

    "Can I afford a car next year?" Bookie answers from your real accounts, budgets and goals.

Features

Everything your money needs. Nothing it doesn't.

An assistant, not a ledger

Chat with Bookie about spending, goals and plans. It can explain a number, categorize a transaction or run a what-if for you.

How much did I spend on food this month?
₦86,400 so far, 18% less than last month. Mostly weekday lunches.
What if I cut that by a third?
Your car fund lands 5 weeks earlier. Want me to set a food budget?
🏦

Every account, one view

Bank, wallet, cash and business accounts, each with its own balance and history.

🌍

Truly multi-currency

Keep KRW, NGN and USD accounts in their own currency. See it all together in yours, with live FX.

⚖️

Honest balances

Bookie shows how confident it is in each balance. Reconcile with the real figure and it corrects the drift.

📊

Budgets that warn early

Category budgets that tell you when you're about to overspend, not after.

🎯

Goals with a plan

A car, rent, an income target. See progress, a projected date, and a heads-up when a goal is at risk.

🔮

What-if scenarios

Earn more, spend less, move a deadline. See the effect on every goal before you commit.
